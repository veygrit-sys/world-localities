# Source attribution and limits

Road names: Taiwan Ministry of the Interior, Department of Household Registration, **全國路名資料**, ROC year 115 (2026), [data.gov.tw dataset 35321](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/35321), retrieved 2026-11-04. The canonical CSV and an audit-only API first-page observation are reproduced in lossless gzip form.

Postal-area labels and three-digit prefixes: Chunghwa Post, [dataset 5949](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/5949), published source `County_h_10906.xml` (2020-06-12), via the existing official/TW registry. Bundled snapshots do not change that source's effective date.

These sources are provided under the [Open Government Data License, version 1.0 (Taiwan)](https://data.gov.tw/license). Retain source attribution and this notice in redistributed extracts. Generated record identifiers, normalized rows and candidate linkage statuses are repository transformations, not statements or endorsements by the source authorities.

English postal-area labels are not English road names. Postal prefixes are not complete six-digit codes. A road-name listing or label candidate is not evidence that a specific house number exists or an address is deliverable. No national completeness, freshness, geocoding, administrative-boundary or delivery guarantee is asserted.

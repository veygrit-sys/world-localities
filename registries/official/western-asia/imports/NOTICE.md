# Source attribution and reuse conditions

Acquired/reviewed: 2026-11-08. Normalization, source-layer indexing and candidate-name comparison were performed by World Localities. These transformations are not endorsed by the original publishers. No new blanket data licence is applied.

## GeoNames

The separate GeoNames country archives remain under their existing CC BY 4.0 notices in `registries/asia/`. Original `gn:` IDs, country fields and source snapshots are unchanged. Search-index rows are not new official address certifications.

## Cyprus Post

Cyprus Post / Republic of Cyprus, National Open Data Portal: Post Code Directory and Postal Codes by Municipality/Community and District. Catalogue URLs and respective 2024 / 2021 temporal coverage are in `manifest.json`. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Changes: source-sheet parsing and separate street, postal-area, government-address and P.O. box records. Source languages and source values are retained.

## Geostat

Source: National Statistics Office of Georgia (Geostat). Population by regions and self-governed units; population by cities and boroughs, series through 2026. Geostat permits data/document reuse with source attribution, including commercial use, subject to its third-party image/logo exclusions. Conditions: https://www.geostat.ge/en/page/monacemta-gamoyenebis-pirobebi . These are statistical labels and time series, not authenticated administrative identity or postcodes.

## Israel Population and Immigration Authority

Source: Population and Immigration Authority, locality list published for free public use through data.gov.il. The catalogue declares `other-open`; its captured metadata is retained in `evidence/`. Publisher city/region codes and jurisdiction claims are stored without territorial reclassification or conversion to postal codes. Blank English names remain blank.

## Jordan Post

Source: Jordan Post / Jordan Open Government Data Portal, 2023 office directory, catalogue updated 2025-07-16. The catalogue declares Open Jordanian License / `JORDONIAN-DEFAULT`. The actual official licence page is https://opendata.gov.jo/en/pages/jordanian-open-government-data-license . The catalogue's erroneous Instagram licence URL is retained as a metadata issue, not adopted as a licence. Changes: skip explanatory preamble, preserve original Arabic office names, codes, governorate and original row fields. An office postcode does not certify a townwide or building assignment.

## Bahrain

Source: Bahrain Survey and Land Registration Bureau / Information & eGovernment Authority (iGA), Area by Governorate, downloaded 2026-11-08 from www.data.gov.bh. Data is governed by the Bahrain Government Open Data Terms at https://www.data.gov.bh/pages/terms-and-conditions/ (also cited by the publisher under its legacy `/en/TermsOfUse` path). Transformations were made by World Localities and are not attributed to the iGA.

The publisher disclaims liability for damage or loss from use of its datasets or derived applications, to the extent allowed by law. Downstream distributions must preserve the source/date attribution, distinguish their transformations, retain the terms and liability notice, and pass those requirements to sublicensees. The terms also require removal upon an applicable iGA request or breach. Consult the captured full terms in `evidence/BH-terms.html.gz`. Old Central-governorate observations are historical; only the latest-period four governorate observations are labelled as such.
